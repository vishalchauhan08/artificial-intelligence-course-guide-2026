Understanding Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are programmed to think and learn. This encompasses a variety of subfields, including machine learning, natural language processing, robotics, and computer vision. The aim is to create systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognizing patterns, and making decisions.

With the explosion of data and the increasing capability of computational power, the demand for AI solutions is unprecedented.
